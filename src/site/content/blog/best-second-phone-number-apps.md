A second phone number app gives you an extra number on the phone you already carry. People use them to separate work from personal life, to sell online without sharing their real number, to get a number in another country, or to run a small business from a smartphone.

There are dozens of these apps, and most "best of" lists are ranked by who paid for placement. This one is different: instead of crowning a winner, we lay out the criteria that matter, describe popular apps fairly based on how they're generally positioned, and suggest which kind of app suits which person.

We've written this from general knowledge as of late 2026. Features and prices change often, so check each app's current pricing page before you commit. DIGIRINGO is our product, and we've tried to be equally honest about where it fits and where it doesn't.

> **Key takeaway:** Pick a second-number app based on what you'll actually do with it. Privacy and short-term use favours burner-style apps; a business favours apps with registered texting, reliable calling, and numbers that stay yours.

## How we compared the apps

We looked at each app against the same six questions:

1. **Purpose:** is it built for privacy, personal use, or business?
2. **Texting:** can you text reliably, and is there support for US business texting registration (A2P 10DLC)?
3. **Numbers:** US only, or Canada, toll-free and other countries too?
4. **Devices:** iPhone, Android, browser?
5. **Team use:** can more than one person use a number?
6. **Pricing transparency:** is it clear what you'll pay each month?

We didn't run lab tests or measure call quality, so you won't find scores here. Call quality depends heavily on your own connection.

## What to look for in a second-number app

Whatever you choose, these features make the difference day to day:

- **The number stays yours.** Some free apps reclaim numbers if you don't use them for a while. For a business, that's a serious risk.
- **Reliable incoming calls.** Look for push notifications that ring your phone even when the app is in the background.
- **Forwarding.** If you miss the call in the app, can it forward to your regular mobile?
- **Voicemail** that's easy to find.
- **Business texting registration** if you'll text US customers for business.
- **Caller ID choice,** so outgoing calls show your second number, not your personal one.
- **Clear pricing** for numbers, minutes, texts and international use.
- **Support** you can actually reach.

## Free vs paid apps: the trade-offs

Free second-number apps are tempting, and for some uses they're fine. But "free" usually comes with conditions.

| Factor | Typical free app | Typical paid app |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Nothing, or ads | Monthly fee or pay-as-you-go |
| Number retention | May be reclaimed if inactive | Kept while you pay |
| Business texting | Often not supported | Often supported with registration |
| Ads | Common | Rare |
| Support | Help articles | Email, chat or phone |
| International numbers | Rare | More common |

For personal privacy, like listing something online, a free or burner app is often enough. For anything customers will rely on, paying a few dollars a month is cheap insurance.

## Best picks by use case

Instead of one winner, here's who each type of app tends to suit.

### Freelancers and solo professionals

You want a stable number, good voicemail, and calls on your existing phone. Google Voice works for many US freelancers on light usage. If you need business texting, international clients or pay-as-you-go pricing, look at business-focused apps like DIGIRINGO, Line2 or Grasshopper.

### Small teams

If several people answer the same number, shared inboxes and team seats matter most. Team-focused platforms like Quo are built around that. DIGIRINGO rings every signed-in device at once and forwards unanswered calls to any cellphone, but proper multi-user seats and a shared inbox are still coming soon.

### International use

If you need a number in another country, or call abroad often, check country coverage and per-minute rates. Privacy apps like Hushed offer numbers in a range of countries; DIGIRINGO offers numbers in many countries (some need ID or address documents) and calling to 220+ countries with rates in the [rate list](/rates). Our guide to [international virtual numbers](/blog/international-virtual-numbers) explains document requirements.

### Privacy and temporary needs

For online marketplaces, dating, or a short project, burner-style apps that let you create and discard numbers quickly are designed for exactly this.

## How to switch later

Your first choice doesn't have to be permanent. To keep the switch painless:

1. **Find out if your number can be ported** before you commit to any app. Some free apps don't allow it.
2. **Run both numbers for a while** if porting isn't possible, forwarding the old one to the new.
3. **Update your listings and signatures** in one go.
4. **Tell regular contacts** with a short text from the old number.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best app for a second phone number?

There isn't one best app for everyone. Privacy apps suit short-term use, team platforms suit groups, and pay-as-you-go business apps suit solo owners and small businesses with uneven usage.

### Are free second number apps safe for business?

They can work for light use, but check whether the number can be reclaimed, whether business texting is supported, and whether you can port the number out later.

### Can I get a second number on an iPhone?

Yes. Many apps have iPhone apps. DIGIRINGO doesn't have an iOS app yet, but iPhone users can use the DIGIRINGO web app in Safari.

### Do second number apps work without Wi-Fi?

Most app-based numbers work over mobile data as well as Wi-Fi. Call quality depends on the strength of your connection.

### Can I text from a second number app?

Most apps support texting. For business texting to US numbers, choose one that supports A2P 10DLC registration so carriers don't filter your messages.
